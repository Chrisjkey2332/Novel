# Quality Analysis: A Walk in the Valley of Shadows

Full manuscript review (Chapters 1-30). Sourced from original `quality_analysis.txt`.

## Priority 1: Plot Holes and Continuity Errors (13 items)

- [ ] **1.01** Chapter 18 titled "Chapter Seventeen" in header. Should be "Chapter Eighteen: The Goodbye."
- [ ] **1.02** Elijah's name introduction missing. Chapters 1-6 use "the hunter"; Chapter 7 switches to "Elijah" without an introduction scene.
- [ ] **1.03** "Three centuries" vs "these months" inconsistency (Ch. 1). Cordelia's shift has "grown thin over these months" but she's lived there for centuries.
- [ ] **1.04** Cave preparation timeline (Ch. 3 vs Ch. 4). Prepared sanctuary vs improvised rescue location needs reconciliation.
- [ ] **1.05** Hunter's authority to execute (Ch. 6). Elijah's judicial authority not established before he exercises it.
- [ ] **1.06** Timeline arithmetic (Ch. 12). Travel day counts don't add up between chapters.
- [ ] **1.07** Children's departure compressed (Ch. 18-19). Each child deserves a distinct goodbye moment.
- [ ] **1.08** Skerren creatures appear without setup (Ch. 19-20). No foreshadowing.
- [ ] **1.09** Corruption creature lacks foreshadowing (Ch. 20-23). Needs ambient references in earlier chapters.
- [ ] **1.10** Unbinding/rebinding mechanics (Ch. 21). Trust justification and voluntary rebinding need explicit address.
- [ ] **1.11** Chapter 30 ends without verdict. Intentionality of open ending needs clearer signal.
- [ ] **1.12** Burned village perpetrators thread left dangling. Archbishop connection unresolved.
- [ ] **1.13** Royal authority vs Church authority unclear. Affects tribunal legitimacy.

## Priority 2: Character Consistency (8 items)

- [ ] **2.01** Cordelia's emotional arc accelerates too quickly. Clinical lens should dominate through Ch. 4-8.
- [ ] **2.02** Clinical lens should persist longer. Full emotional shift should not complete until Ch. 15-20.
- [ ] **2.03** "Something I cannot name" overused. Should progress from inability to tentative naming to certainty.
- [ ] **2.04** Elijah's "doubt" in Ch. 14. Frame as discernment, not crisis, to protect spiritual consistency.
- [ ] **2.05** Elijah's emotional expression in Ch. 29. Should read as depth of feeling through composure, not breakdown.
- [ ] **2.06** Martha's maturity level. Some insights too analytically sophisticated for a traumatized twelve-year-old.
- [ ] **2.07** Lily's speaking moment (Ch. 18). Needs physical buildup in preceding chapters.
- [ ] **2.08** Individual child voices blur after Ch. 19. James, Samuel, Peter, and Wren need distinct moments.
