## Beat-Sheet Adherence

The manuscript hits all 10 beats in roughly the right order. Notes on each:

- **Beat 1 — The Capture (Ch. 1–2):** Lands precisely on schedule. No drift.
- **Beat 2 — The Children Revealed (Ch. 3–5):** Lands. Ch. 3's bridge function is the only fragility (see P5 #1).
- **Beat 3 — The Village Trial (Ch. 6):** Lands cleanly. Strongest single chapter in Part One.
- **Beat 4 — The Law and the Journey (Ch. 7–8):** Lands with the most drag in Part One. See P5 #2, #3.
- **Beat 5 — The Road and Growing Understanding (Ch. 9–11):** Lands but the shape has drifted — it actually lands in Ch. 11, not distributed across 9–11. Ch. 9 is thin (see P5 #4); Ch. 10 is dense; Ch. 11 carries the beat.
- **Beat 6 — The Burned Village and the Werefolk (Ch. 12–17):** Lands at full force. Longest beat in the book, earned throughout. Lily's "Come back" payoff is in Ch. 18 — slightly outside the JSON's specified range — but the landing position is structurally correct.
- **Beat 7 — The Nature of Monsters (Ch. 18–22):** Lands. The "two weeks of healing" the beat-sheet promises actually happens in Ch. 23–24, and the "share their full backstories" lands in Ch. 25 — beat content has migrated forward by 1–3 chapters.
- **Beat 8 — The Holy See (Ch. 23–26):** Drifted. The city doesn't appear until Ch. 26 (chapter 4 of 8 in Part Three). Ch. 23–25 are still Part Two's denouement structurally. The beat-sheet's "systematic humiliation" lands in Ch. 27, which the JSON assigns to Beat 9.
- **Beat 9 — Blood and Cages (Ch. 27–28):** Lands. Ch. 27 carries Beat 8's humiliation material; Ch. 28 is Seraine's visit, one of the most controlled chapters in the book.
- **Beat 10 — The Final Night and Judgment (Ch. 29–30):** Lands. The Archbishop reveal in Ch. 30 has been recently polished (per commits `3b837d2`, `96e6ae7`) and arrives with appropriate weight. The beat-sheet's "His mask of strength breaks. He walks away small and broken" is more restrained in execution — Elijah is changed but not broken — and the executed version is the stronger one.

**Net assessment:** Beats 7→8→9 have re-distributed across the manuscript. The narrative works; the JSON now misrepresents where the beats land. Recommend updating `beat-sheet.json` to reflect actual chapter assignments (Beat 8 = Ch. 26 only; Beat 9 = Ch. 27–28; the healing/backstory material of Beats 7-8 boundary lives in Ch. 23–25). This is documentation maintenance, not a manuscript change.

## Five-Attempts Arc Evaluation

The arc currently lands as four distinct verbal attempts plus the silence, with two divergences from the JSON.

- **Attempt 1 (Ch. 9 — "Practical Argument" per JSON):** The JSON's specified exchange ("You know they will execute me. Why waste your time?" / "You will receive a trial") **does not appear in the manuscript.** What's there instead is Cordelia musing about losing herself in the forest (lines 14–22) and Elijah asking "Could you, though? Lose yourself entirely?" Interior rather than dialogic; testing herself rather than testing him.
- **Attempt 2 (Ch. 11 — "Freedom Argument"):** Lands. The ridge-viewpoint setting and the jokes/tropes parallel reveal both serve the attempt. **The strongest of the four.**
- **Attempt 3 (Ch. 15 — "Personal Appeal" per JSON):** **Missing from Ch. 15.** The personal appeal ("Come with me, Elijah. Leave them.") actually appears at the end of Ch. 14, layered on top of the Sera-interrogation climax.
- **Attempt 4 (Ch. 18 per JSON, actually Ch. 19 — "Desperate Argument"):** Lands. The skerren-pass aftermath ("Come with me. ... You are better than the system you serve") is the attempt's clearest articulation. **The most dramatically pressurized.**
- **Attempt 5 (Ch. 22 — "The Silence"):** Lands. The choosing-not-to-leave on the ridge (Ch. 23) functions as confirmation.

**Verdict:** The arc has accelerated and the attempts have grown more sophisticated than the JSON specifies, but a reader counting attempts will find four, not five. The arc is artistically stronger now; the structural promise is weaker. See P5 #8 for resolution options.

## Part Transitions

**Ch. 8 → Ch. 9 (Part One → Part Two):** Under-prepared. Ch. 8 ends on a strong emotional close (Lily's recognition of the rook's prayer-symbolism). But Ch. 9 opens with a six-week time-skip handled in three paragraphs of summary and immediately bolts on a night-terror climax. The reader is told that significant time has passed, given the children's healing as summary, and then dropped into a present-tense crisis that has no setup. **Primarily a Ch. 9 problem.** See P5 #4.

**Ch. 22 → Ch. 23 (Part Two → Part Three):** Earned. Ch. 22 ends mid-crisis (the wendigo has Elijah, the glass breaks) and Ch. 23 opens directly in the resolution — a single continuous action across the chapter break. Part Three begins not with the Holy See but with the wendigo's death and the choice-to-stay sequence. This is a *thematic* part transition (Cordelia's transformation completes) rather than a *physical* one, and the execution is better than the plan.

## Summary

**Outlier chapters (verdict on length):**
- **Ch. 3 (1,628):** Too short. Function as bridge. Absorb into Ch. 4 (recommended) or Ch. 2.
- **Ch. 8 (6,185):** Reads as two chapters glued. Compression possible.
- **Ch. 9 (2,641):** Pacing problem more than length. Restructure the time-skip and move the night-terror.
- **Ch. 14 (8,200):** Length earned; trim ~1,000 words of middle-chapter analytical digressions.
- **Ch. 21 (7,881):** Length necessary for unbinding; opening meditation can shed 800–1,200 words.
- **Ch. 23 (7,874):** Length necessary for choice-to-stay; both pre-turning and post-turning sequences iterate.

**Part Two middle-sag:** Not present in the conventional sense. The chapters most vulnerable to "is this earning its place" are Ch. 9 (thin opener), Ch. 10 (three-meditations-on-faith density), Ch. 16 (werefolk camp scene-painting). The Shepherd reveal in Ch. 17 resets momentum decisively. **No intervention required.** If tightening Part Two is wanted, Ch. 9 (restructure) and Ch. 16 (trim) are the most economical targets.

**Top three pacing fixes recommended before 1.2:**

1. **Resolve Ch. 3's structural status.** A 1,628-word chapter ending on a hook is functioning as a scene, not a chapter. Absorb into Ch. 4 (recommended) or expand to standalone-status length. Single most impactful structural choice available.
2. **Restructure the Ch. 8 → Ch. 9 transition.** Move the night-terror climax to open Ch. 10 so Ch. 9 closes quietly on "Tomorrow we will walk again." Part Two gets a more deliberate opening and the night-terror gets the setup it deserves.
3. **Resolve the five-attempts arc / beat-sheet divergence.** Either update `references/plot/five-attempts.json` and `beat-sheet.json` to match the manuscript's actual structure, or insert the missing Attempt 1 and Attempt 3 dialogue exchanges. Recommend updating the reference docs — the manuscript's version is artistically the better one and the structural promise of "five attempts" is invisible to readers without the JSON in hand.

**New pacing issues not in the 2026-02-17 list:**
- Ch. 3's chapter-status (above).
- The Ch. 8 → Ch. 9 part transition asymmetry.
- Ch. 21's opening-meditation length.
- The five-attempts arc's drift from JSON.
- Beat-sheet drift at Beats 7-8-9 boundaries.
