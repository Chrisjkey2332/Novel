# Ash and Oath — Chapter Plans, Part Two: The Choice and the Ash

Detailed scene-level plans for chapters 20–28 (Part Two), following the beat sheet and chapter map. Part Two falls fast: the caravan's return and the war-summons force Tomas's choice; he refuses Soraya's plea and chooses the oath; the hyenadin slaughter the fold in his absence; he turns back too late and is unmade, arriving to find her last among the ash. Dual POV (Tomas / Soraya). Target ~3,500–4,000 words/chapter.

---

## Chapter 20: The Caravan Returns
- **POV**: Tomas
- **Beat**: 9 (rising to Pinch 2 / midpoint pivot)
- **Goal**: Wat returns with resources and the war-summons; the deal is fulfilled; the choice detonates on a bonded man who has found his home.
- **Scenes**:
  1. Wat rides in with the caravan — reunion; the price delivered, the bargain fulfilled, Tomas free to go. Wat's joy, and his willful blindness to his changed hero: "we can go home now, Ser."
  2. The summons — the crown's word: the Beastkin and Geistkin empires slide toward war, and Marrenhold needs its guarantors home. The duty side now carries a whole kingdom's survival.
  3. The detonation — Tomas, bonded, of the fold, married to Soraya, realizes the payment does not free him: it forces the choice. The fold begins to ready itself to move.
- **Ends with**: The choice made real and inescapable — the home he found against the summons he owes.

## Chapter 21: The Summons Falls
- **POV**: Soraya
- **Beat**: 9
- **Goal**: Her side — the fold preparing to move; the war-news's weight; her dread; the hyenadin first glimpsed.
- **Scenes**:
  1. The fold receives the resources and readies to strike camp — the bittersweet ritual of a people who may never stay.
  2. Soraya and Tomas — the unspoken question now between them: will he come, or go? Her dread; her wound whispering that to keep him is to destroy him.
  3. The first hyenadin sign — a scout, tracks, a distant pack; her deep-sense of the ground feels the wrongness; the god's warning echoes.
- **Ends with**: The fold on the verge of moving, the choice looming — and something circling in the dark.

## Chapter 22: The Weight
- **POV**: Tomas
- **Beat**: 10 (rising to Pinch 2)
- **Goal**: The agony of the choice — oath, Rowan, Wat, and Marrenhold against the fold, his wife, and the only home he has known.
- **Scenes**:
  1. Tomas torn — the summons and Rowan (the maker-father he owes everything), the oath (to break it is to be unmade), Marrenhold's survival; against Soraya and the fold.
  2. Wat's pull — the boy, unable to see the love, presses homeward ("we're needed, Ser"); his innocent devotion a blade pointed at duty.
  3. Tomas wrestles it alone, or with Vesh or Sela — and Sela's foresight surfaces, a fragment of the coming disaster he cannot yet grasp.
- **Ends with**: Tomas unable to choose, the deadline closing, the fold nearly ready to depart.

## Chapter 23: The Circling
- **POV**: Soraya
- **Beat**: 10
- **Goal**: The hyenadin gather nearer; the god's warning looms; her fear for Tomas and the fold.
- **Scenes**:
  1. The menace grows — closer signs, a scare or a lost outrider; the fold's defense-only vulnerability made vivid (they cannot strike back without deepening the curse).
  2. Soraya and Kaelen — the danger and their few options; the guilt between them stirring; the burden of leading the doomed.
  3. Soraya and Tomas — she senses he leans toward leaving; her wound and her love at war; she begins to steel herself either to let him go or to ask him to stay.
- **Ends with**: The threat gathering; the choice about to break.

## Chapter 24: She Asks
- **POV**: Soraya
- **Beat**: 11 (Pinch Point 2)
- **Goal**: She breaks a lifetime's discipline and asks him to stay; he refuses; he leaves.
- **Scenes**:
  1. The asking — Soraya, who has wanted nothing she cannot carry, breaks and asks Tomas to stay; the most she has ever risked, her whole self in it.
  2. The refusal — Tomas, whose whole self is his kept word, chooses the oath; his reasons (Rowan, the realm, the summons, the belief that he must); the anguish, and the refusal.
  3. The leaving — he departs the mesa with Wat, his refusal the last thing between them; Soraya watching him go.
- **Ends with**: Tomas gone; Soraya alone with the refusal — and the dark closing in.

## Chapter 25: The Fall of the Fold
- **POV**: Soraya
- **Beat**: 12 (Resolution — the catastrophe)
- **Goal**: The hyenadin attack; her stand; Kaelen's fire and death; the reckoning; she falls, last of the fold.
- **Scenes**:
  1. The attack — the hyenadin fall on the mesa in Tomas's absence; the defense-bound fold is overwhelmed; Soraya fights with the divine armaments the bond granted her; Ravan, Vesh, and Sela caught in the chaos — named losses.
  2. Kaelen breaks — a hundred years of restraint spent at last; he looses catastrophic fire, annihilating attackers as the curse withers him to death on the black earth; father and daughter reckon and forgive.
  3. Soraya's last stand and death — she falls defending her people, the last of the fold, dying without ever knowing Tomas turned back; through the bond, her death reaches him far off.
- **Ends with**: The fold dead, Soraya gone, the mesa silent under climbing smoke.

## Chapter 26: The Smoke
- **POV**: Tomas
- **Beat**: 13 (Plot Turn 2 — the futile turn)
- **Goal**: The bond tears; he orders Wat on; forsakes the summons and is unmade; races back.
- **Scenes**:
  1. On the road — the bond tears; Tomas feels Soraya die, a wound opening in his own soul; he turns and sees smoke climbing from the mesa behind him.
  2. Wat ordered on — his last act as a knight: he commands Wat to ride on to Marrenhold with word of the war, sending the boy to safety and duty; Wat's anguish.
  3. The unmaking — Tomas abandons the summons; forsaking it breaks the faith-oath, which strips the knighthood, the name, the standing, until only nobody-Tomas remains.
- **Ends with**: Tomas riding into the smoke — unmade, and too late.

## Chapter 27: The Mesa
- **POV**: Tomas
- **Beat**: 14 (Resolution — the aftermath)
- **Goal**: The aftermath; he reads the last stand; finds Soraya last; the severed bond maims him.
- **Scenes**:
  1. The aftermath — Tomas walks the slaughtered camp: scorched attackers, Kaelen withered on the black earth (his fire spent), the fold dead among the ash; named losses (Vesh, Sela, Ravan).
  2. The search — he moves through the dead, dreading and hoping, the horror mounting.
  3. Soraya, last — he finds her body last; the severed bond's catastrophic, permanent wound takes him. He does not die; he is maimed, body and soul.
- **Ends with**: Tomas on his knees with his wife in his arms among the ash.

## Chapter 28: Ash and Oath
- **POV**: Tomas
- **Beat**: 14 (Resolution — coda)
- **Goal**: The final image; forsworn, unmade, maimed; the tragedy complete.
- **Scenes**:
  1. The stillness after — Tomas among the dead; what little remains of him; the enormity of the loss: love, people, oath, and self, all gone.
  2. The futility — the knowledge that he came back and it changed nothing; the god's blessing and warning both fulfilled.
  3. The closing image — a forsworn, unmade, maimed nobody among the ash with his dead wife; the road, the smoke, the silence. Ash, and oath.
- **Ends with**: The book's final beat at the mesa — no return to Marrenhold; Rowan's grief only implied, off the page.
