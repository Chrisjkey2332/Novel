# Ash and Oath — Voice & Style Guide

The locked voice specification for the manuscript. *Ash and Oath* is dual-POV, and the two POV characters carry **two deliberately distinct voices** — the head-switch alone should tell a reader whose chapter they are in. Keep every chapter true to its POV's voice.

## House rules (both POVs)
- **Tense / person:** third-person limited, **past tense**.
- **POV assignment:** by chapter, per the chapter map (organic, weighted; ~15 Tomas / 13 Soraya).
- **Baseline → peaks:** hold each voice at its normal register through ordinary scenes, and **deepen toward raw immediacy at the emotional peaks** (the Blaze, the bonding/trial, the refusal, the Fall, the mesa) — closer distance, barer sentences.
- **World diction:** elevated-but-plain and faintly timeless; no modern anachronisms. Use the people-names **the Emberkin, the Vharr, the Niven**; the tribe is the fold.
- **Punctuation — NO em-dashes.** Do not use the em-dash ("—") or the spaced hyphen (" - ") for parentheticals, appositives, summaries, or interruptions. Restructure instead: split into separate sentences (period), use a comma, a colon (for a summary or reveal), a semicolon (for two linked clauses), or parentheses (used sparingly). For interrupted or trailing dialogue, end the line or use an ellipsis rather than a dash. This applies to the whole manuscript and all future drafting.
- **The core contrast:** **Tomas hides; Soraya carries.** His voice is *mind* — reason, control, accountability. Hers is *body and heart* — feeling, intuition, surrender.

---

## Tomas — "the Warrant voice": cut stone
The prose of a guarded, disciplined man whose whole self is his kept word, and who conceals his heart behind his title.

- **Distance:** mid-close; often "the knight" / "Ser Warrant"; interiority held at arm's length — the narration conceals him as he conceals himself.
- **Diction:** elevated, spare, faintly archaic; abstract nouns — *honor, cost, word, weight*.
- **Rhythm:** long **periodic** sentences that resolve on a short, hard beat; triads; em-dashes; disciplined.
- **Figuration:** sparing, precise, thematic — erosion, scars, coins, binding.
- **Tone:** grave, dry-ironic, restrained. **Emotion is implied through control, never named.**
- **Signature move:** the aphorism. He *concludes*, and he stops at the part of the truth he can afford.

**Touchstone (the fold at evening):**
> By the second week the knight had learned the shape of their evenings. The fold made camp the way disciplined men struck one — without waste, without complaint, each to a task no one had to name — and that, more than the scales or the bound wings, was what undid the story he had been sent to carry. Monsters did not ration their grief. Monsters did not teach their children to fold a tent in silence so the small ones could sleep. He watched, and kept his face still, and told himself he was only gathering what a guarantor gathers. It was true. It was not all of the truth, but he had long practice in stopping at the part that was.

## Soraya — "the earth voice": feeling-led and soft
The prose of a woman who feels everything and answers for none of it — intuitive, tender, unguarded. Emotionally the opposite of Tomas.

- **Register:** emotional and intuitive, **not reasoned**. She *feels and reaches*; she does not deduce, weigh, or hold herself to account. Avoid cause-and-effect logic, self-blame, and aphorism in her narration.
- **Distance:** very close; "Soraya" / "she," inhabited; feeling-first, and the ache leaks through.
- **Diction:** tender, warm, sensory — the heart and the body; *shy, sweet, cool, ache, undone*; gentler sounds.
- **Rhythm:** flowing and yielding, carried by feeling and association; it **opens and spills** rather than snapping shut. No hard reasoning-clamps.
- **Tone:** vulnerable, yearning, open; she lets herself be moved and neither explains nor excuses it.
- **Signature move:** sensation over conclusion. Where Tomas concludes, **she touches.**

**Touchstone (the same moment — the doomed green):**
> The green always came to her like something shy. It rose where she pressed her palm to the grey — a first blade, then three, then a soft cool fistful nodding up between her fingers — and her whole heart went to them at once, helplessly, the way it went to anything small and living and warm for the length of a breath. She gathered them against her skin. She did not think of after; she never could, not while they were still curling toward her, sweet and green and trusting. There was only this: the coolness in her hand, the tender ache blooming under her ribs, the wanting that filled her up and up and asked for nothing, and left her, every time, a little more undone.

**Guardrail:** her *narration* is feeling-led and unaccountable, but her *actions* stay decisive where the plot needs them (she is the heir; she breaks her silence to ask him to stay). She reads as deeply feeling, never passive.

## Contrast at a glance
| | Tomas | Soraya |
|---|---|---|
| Mode | reason, control, accountability | feeling, intuition, surrender |
| Renders experience as | a truth to conclude | a sensation to be moved by |
| Toward herself/himself | self-judging, self-hiding | unguarded, unaccounting |
| Sentences | snap shut on a hard beat | open and spill |
| Images | scars, coins, iron | root, soil, doomed green |
| Emotion | hidden, implied | carried, leaking through |
